# Message from the CEO

**Greetings from the CEO & Owner of Red Rocks Fire Protection Services.**

Hi, I’m Brian Kakac, CEO & Owner of Red Rocks Fire Protection Services.

Since 2010, we’ve proudly served property owners and managers across Colorado, helping protect what matters most: your employees, your residents, your tenants, your properties, your buildings, and your peace of mind. We live here, we work here, and we’re deeply committed to protecting the communities we serve like they’re our own. I would love the opportunity to earn your business.

Red Rocks Fire Protection Services was built alongside my father, John Kakac, whose leadership, work ethic, and commitment to people helped shape the foundation of this company from the very beginning. He believed in doing things the right way, treating people with respect, standing behind your work, and always showing up for others. Those values continue to guide how we operate every day.

Although my father has since passed, his influence remains deeply woven into our company, our team culture, and the way we serve our customers. It is a privilege to carry forward the vision we built together and continue growing a company rooted in family values, accountability, hard work, and genuine care for people.

At Red Rocks Fire Protection Services, our purpose is simple, but incredibly important: we exist to protect lives and property.

We also believe it is both a responsibility and a privilege to serve not only our customers, but also the employees and team members who help make this company what it is today. We have worked hard to build a culture where people support one another, work together, and take pride in doing meaningful work that truly impacts the safety of others.

When you partner with us, you’re not just hiring a fire protection company, you’re gaining a team that understands the responsibility you carry every day.

We built our company around a One Vendor Life Safety Solution, giving you one trusted partner to manage inspections, service, repairs, and compliance.

We understand your challenges: tight timelines, evolving regulations, and the responsibility of keeping people safe. Our job is to simplify that process and give you confidence.

We are grateful for the trust our customers place in us and proud of the long-term relationships we continue to build throughout Colorado. Every opportunity to serve is something we never take for granted.

If you’re looking for a responsive, experienced partner who shows up when it matters most, we’re ready and would love the opportunity to earn your business.

Sincerely,

Brian Kakac – CEO & Owner, Red Rocks Fire Protection Services
