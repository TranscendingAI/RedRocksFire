# Why We Exist (Our Core Purpose)

**We exist to protect lives and property by simplifying life safety compliance and serving as a proactive partner for property owners and managers.**

At Red Rocks Fire Protection Services, we believe safety is not optional—and delivering it is both a responsibility and a privilege. We take that responsibility seriously, approaching every system, every service, and every client relationship with a deep sense of duty and passion. Our purpose is to bring clarity, reliability, and confidence to every client we serve.

# Our Mission

To protect lives and property by delivering proactive, reliable, and fully integrated life safety solutions, while guiding clients through compliance with clarity and accountability.

# Our Vision

To become the most trusted life safety partner in Colorado, recognized for simplifying compliance, protecting communities, and delivering unmatched service.

# Our Core Values

At Red Rocks Fire Protection Services, our Core Values are more than words, they are the standards that guide every decision, every interaction, and every commitment we make to our customers, employees, and community.

These values define how we operate as a company, how we support one another as a team, and how we fulfill our responsibility to protect lives and property every day.

## Safety First — Always

Safety is at the center of everything we do. We believe protecting lives and property is both a responsibility and a privilege. Every system, service call, inspection, and interaction is approached with a deep sense of duty, accountability, and care for the people who depend on us.

## Advocacy for Our Clients

We believe our responsibility goes beyond providing life safety services, we serve as proactive advocates for our clients. We stay ahead of evolving fire codes, legislation, compliance requirements, and jurisdictional changes so we can help guide, educate, and protect the people and properties that depend on us. Through clear communication, trusted guidance, and long-term partnership, we help our clients navigate complexity with confidence.

## Go the Extra Mile

We take personal ownership to make sure the job is done right, the customer is informed, and nothing is left unresolved. We believe reliability is non-negotiable, and we remain committed until every issue is fully addressed and every customer feels supported.

## Character

We do the right thing—always—by acting with integrity, respect, accountability, and professionalism. Character is reflected in how we communicate, honor our commitments, perform our work, and represent RRFPS every day. We hold ourselves to a high standard and stand behind our work, creating trust with our customers, strengthening our team, and contributing to the long-term success of our company.

## One Family

We believe the strongest outcomes happen when people embrace a **team above self** mindset, support one another, collaborate openly, and work toward a common purpose. We treat our employees, customers, and partners like family, supporting one another with respect, care, and accountability. This value reflects the foundation John and Brian Kakac built from the very beginning: a culture of trust, teamwork, mentorship, and genuine care for people.
