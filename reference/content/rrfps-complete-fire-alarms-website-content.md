## Fire Alarm System Service

A properly functioning fire alarm system is critical to protecting lives, property, and business operations. Fire alarm systems provide early warning and notification during an emergency, allowing occupants to evacuate safely and emergency responders to react quickly.

Red Rocks Fire Protection provides comprehensive fire alarm service for commercial, multifamily, healthcare, retail, industrial, and assisted living facilities throughout Colorado. Our certified technicians diagnose system troubles, perform repairs, replace failed devices, troubleshoot communication issues, and maintain system reliability to ensure compliance with NFPA 72 and local fire code requirements.

### Our Fire Alarm Services Include

- Troubleshooting and diagnostics
- Smoke detector replacement
- Heat detector replacement
- Pull station replacement
- Horn and strobe replacement
- Control panel service and repair
- Power supply replacement
- Battery testing and replacement
- Monitoring communication repairs
- Ground fault troubleshooting
- Emergency service calls
- 24/7 emergency response

## Fire Alarm System Modifications

Building renovations, tenant improvements, occupancy changes, and code upgrades often require modifications to existing fire alarm systems.

Red Rocks Fire Protection specializes in designing and installing fire alarm system modifications that maintain proper system functionality and code compliance. Our team works with property owners, management companies, contractors, and local Authorities Having Jurisdiction (AHJs) to ensure all modifications meet applicable codes and project requirements.

### Common Fire Alarm Modifications Include

- Tenant finish projects
- Office remodels
- Device relocations
- Additional smoke detectors
- Additional notification appliances
- New pull stations
- Elevator recall modifications
- Duct detector additions
- New monitoring points
- Building expansions
- Code compliance upgrades

Our team handles the entire process, including design, permitting, installation, programming, testing, and final acceptance.

## Fire Alarm System Replacement

As fire alarm systems age, components become obsolete, replacement parts become difficult to obtain, and reliability can decrease. In many cases, replacing an aging system provides a more cost-effective and dependable long-term solution than continued repairs.

Red Rocks Fire Protection provides complete fire alarm system replacement services, from small control panel upgrades to complete building-wide system replacements.

### Systems and Components We Replace

- Fire alarm control panels
- Remote annunciators
- Power supplies
- Smoke detectors
- Heat detectors
- Pull stations
- Horns and strobes
- Communication equipment
- Monitoring equipment
- Voice evacuation systems
- Emergency responder communication systems (BDA/ERRCS)

### Signs Your System May Need Replacement

- Obsolete equipment
- Discontinued replacement parts
- Frequent trouble conditions
- Recurring service calls
- Failed communication equipment
- Expansion limitations
- Repeated false alarms
- Code compliance concerns
- Aging infrastructure

Our replacement projects are designed to improve reliability, simplify maintenance, and provide long-term life safety protection.
